Skyline City

An 8×8 downtown of Minecraft skyscrapers, tallest in the middle, with merged double lots and superblocks. Every block can be mined or made in bulk; nothing from Chisel or ProjectRed.

Roads & lighting

A highway each way (18 wide, two lanes each side of a barrier), a tree-lined boulevard each way, and streets of 6 or 8. Glowstone light poles line every sidewalk and the highway barriers. Magnum Torches inside the towers stop mobs spawning anywhere in the city: each one covers 128 blocks out but only 32 up and down, so there is a set every 48 blocks of height.

Styles

On single lots: glass office, brick deco, sandstone ziggurat, quartz needle, clay block, twin towers, nether tower, copper deco, electrum & lead, twisting tower, octagon tower, L-block. On double lots: slab tower, podium & towers, gateway. On 2×2 superblocks: landmark supertower, tower cluster. Windows are Extra Utilities Thickened Glass. Metal blocks are Thermal Expansion storage blocks; yellorium is Big Reactors.
